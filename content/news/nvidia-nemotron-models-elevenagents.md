NVIDIA and ElevenLabs expanded their partnership on November 11, 2026, making NVIDIA's open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model available inside ElevenAgents, ElevenLabs' platform for building voice agents. The Nemotron ElevenAgents news came at ElevenLabs' New York summit, together with plans for more on-premises compute and a sovereign voice agent deployment for Poland's national health service.

For buyers, the practical change is another model option inside a major voice agent platform, one that ElevenLabs says is both fast and cheap for customer-support style work.

## What was announced

ElevenLabs' announcement covers three areas: models, infrastructure and sovereign deployments.

**Models.** Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is an open NVIDIA model designed for agent tasks such as reasoning, calling tools and working through multi-step requests. Inside ElevenAgents it pairs with ElevenLabs' low-latency, multilingual voice models. A few specifics from the company:

- Reasoning can be turned off for simpler tasks, which ElevenLabs says puts the model among the three fastest on ElevenAgents.
- The model can be post-trained on enterprise data, and customers can use those tuned versions in ElevenAgents.
- On ElevenLabs' internal benchmark of realistic customer-support tasks, the company says the model reached leading end-to-end task completion at 70% lower cost than other leading open models. That is a self-reported result on the company's own test, and the comparison models were not named.

**Infrastructure.** ElevenLabs plans to add 15 MW of NVIDIA Vera Rubin capacity on top of its existing Blackwell Ultra systems. The company says the on-premises capacity is meant for large deployments with strict security or data-residency needs, and for its research teams.

**Sovereign deployment.** The first named example is Poland. ElevenLabs models and NVIDIA infrastructure power a voice agent for Centralna e-Rejestracja, the central appointment system in Poland's national e-Health platform, with delivery support from EasyCall. The agent calls patients who only have landlines, reminds them of appointments and helps them reschedule. ElevenLabs puts the system's volume at about 40 million appointments a year and says it runs on-premises using NVIDIA Confidential Computing, so citizen data and audio stay within government infrastructure.

The companies have worked together since 2022, and NVIDIA became a strategic investor in ElevenLabs in 2025, according to ElevenLabs.

## Why it matters for teams that sell or support by phone

Most voice agents are only as good as the language model making decisions behind the voice. This deal touches the three things buyers ask about most:

| Concern | What this changes |
|---|---|
| Cost per call | Another lower-cost model option for routine support tasks, if the company's benchmark holds up in real use |
| Speed | A reasoning toggle lets simple turns skip slower thinking steps |
| Data control | On-premises capacity and confidential computing for regulated or public-sector buyers |

The Poland project is also a useful reminder that outbound voice agents are moving into reminder and rescheduling work at very large scale, not just inbound support. Appointment reminders and rebooking are close cousins of the [appointment setting](/blog/ai-appointment-setting) work sales teams already automate.

There are limits worth stating. A benchmark designed by the platform vendor is a starting point, not proof, and open models still need careful testing on your own scripts and edge cases before they replace what you run now.
