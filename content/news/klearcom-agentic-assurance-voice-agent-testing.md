Klearcom, an Irish company that tests phone systems for large enterprises, launched Agentic Assurance on November 3, 2026, a product for AI voice agent testing that places real calls over local phone networks. The company tied the launch to a figure, cited in its release, that 52% of US contact centers plan to roll out AI voice agents in 2027. The product is aimed at enterprises that want evidence their agents work for real callers in every market they serve.

## What was announced

Klearcom, based in Waterford, has tested IVRs and voicebots for years and describes AI voice agents as the fourth major migration it has supported, after the moves from PSTN to SIP, on-premise systems to cloud contact centers, and keypad menus to speech IVRs. According to the release, Agentic Assurance:

- places real calls over local fixed and mobile networks, reaching agents the way customers do, across more than 100 countries and 340 carriers (company figures)
- scores agents on more than 100 parameters grouped into audio quality, service quality, compliance and security, and complexity
- tests in more than 20 languages
- checks each market's disclosure, authentication and data-handling requirements and keeps an evidence trail for compliance and audit teams
- runs continuously after launch, not just before go-live

Klearcom's argument is about fault isolation. Carriers, contact center platforms and AI providers each monitor their own systems, so a call can fail while every supplier reports that everything is healthy. Its example is a call made within Germany that may be routed through data centers in several countries before it reaches the agent.

The company's chief technology officer said that a single model or knowledge base update can change an agent's behaviour overnight, which is why testing has to continue for as long as an agent is live. Its chief revenue officer noted that customers blame the brand, not the supplier, when an agent fails.

On the 52% figure: the release links to it but does not name the survey's source, sample size or method, so it is best read as an indication of interest rather than a firm forecast. Klearcom is also not the only company in this space; Cyara, for example, announced agentic testing for voice and IVR AI agents in March 2026.

## Why it matters for teams that sell or support by phone

Most sales teams will not need multi-country carrier testing. The underlying point applies to any team running an AI voice agent, though: an agent that works in a demo can still fail on a real line because of audio quality, latency, a confusing prompt or a knowledge gap.

A lighter version of the same discipline:

| Check | When |
|---|---|
| Call the agent from a real mobile phone, not just a browser | Before every launch |
| Run a fixed set of test scenarios (objections, interruptions, off-topic questions, a request for a human) | Before launch and after any prompt or knowledge change |
| Review a sample of live transcripts each week | Ongoing |
| Confirm the agent discloses it is an AI and names the business | Every test run |
