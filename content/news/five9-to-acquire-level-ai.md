Five9 said on November 5, 2026 that it has agreed to acquire Level AI, a company that sells AI agents and conversation intelligence to contact centers, in a cash deal valuing it at about $325 million. The agreement for Five9 to acquire Level AI was announced alongside the contact center vendor's third-quarter results, and the deal is expected to close before the end of 2026, subject to closing conditions.

It is the second large voice AI deal in a week, after Salesforce agreed to buy Cresta on November 2, and points the same way: contact center and CRM platforms want to own the AI layer that listens to, scores and increasingly handles customer calls.

## What was announced

According to Five9's press release, filed with the SEC:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Announced | November 5, 2026 |
| Deal type | Definitive agreement, approved unanimously by Five9's board |
| Value | About $325 million |
| Payment | Cash from Five9's balance sheet |
| Expected close | During 2026, subject to closing conditions |
| Level AI customers named | Gusto, Chime, Empyrean, VistaPrint |

Level AI's product covers AI agents, agent assist, quality management, voice of the customer analysis, coaching and what it calls AI Workers, all on a single conversation intelligence layer. It runs on Latitude, its own family of models trained for customer conversations, which the company says are cheaper and faster than frontier models and give enterprises more control over their data. Level AI says it processes more than one billion customer interactions a year. These are company figures.

The two companies are not strangers. They have worked together since 2022, and Level AI's apps are already sold through the Five9 CX Marketplace.

## The standalone twist

The most notable part of the plan is distribution. Five9 says Level AI's applications will be sold as standalone products, so a company can buy them without moving to Five9's cloud contact center. That includes businesses still running on-premises systems or another cloud provider. The same apps will also become part of the AI application layer in Five9's Genius CX Platform.

Five9 CEO Amit Mathradas framed the deal around AI and human agents working as one system, and said it gives enterprises a way to adopt AI without first migrating their contact center. Level AI founder and CEO Ashish Nagar said existing customers will continue to be supported.

Five9 has not yet said how overlapping products, such as its own quality management and AI agent tools, will be combined with Level AI's. Five9 says its fourth-quarter guidance does not include any contribution from Level AI.

## Why it matters for teams that sell or support by phone

Three things stand out for anyone running calls at volume:

- **Every call gets scored.** Conversation intelligence that grades 100% of calls, not a sample, is becoming standard in large contact centers. The same idea applies to small teams: review what your agents, human or AI, actually say. Our [call quality scorecard](/resources/call-quality-scorecard) is a simple place to start.
- **Smaller, specialised models.** Level AI's pitch rests on purpose-built models that are cheaper and faster than general frontier models. Expect more vendors to mix specialised and general models to manage cost and latency.
- **Less lock-in, in theory.** Selling AI apps separately from the contact center platform gives buyers more room to mix vendors, though integration quality after a deal closes is what decides that in practice.

## What to watch next

- The closing date and how Level AI's team and products fit into Five9.
- Pricing and packaging for the standalone Level AI apps.
- Whether existing Level AI customers on other platforms see any change in support.
- Further consolidation among contact center vendors, following Salesforce's Cresta deal the same week.
