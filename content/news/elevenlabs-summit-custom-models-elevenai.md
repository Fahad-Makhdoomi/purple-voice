ElevenLabs used its New York summit on November 11, 2026 to launch a batch of enterprise features, led by model customization on customers' own call data and a new workplace app called ElevenAI. The ElevenLabs summit also brought memory and routines for its ElevenAgents voice agent platform, a formal partner program and a marketplace for partner-built agents.

Taken together, the announcements show a voice AI company moving from selling voices and APIs toward selling a full agent platform with an ecosystem around it.

## What was announced

According to ElevenLabs' own summary of the event, the main items were:

| Announcement | What it is | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Model customization | Fine-tuning selected open-source LLMs and ElevenLabs voice models on live deployment data, with ElevenLabs researchers | Coming months |
| Scribe v3 Medical | Speech-to-text tuned for healthcare terms; a financial services model is in the works | Announced |
| Interaction Engine | Handles turn-taking and interruptions, and routes each part of a call to a cheaper or more capable LLM | Preview |
| Agent memory | Agents keep a structured record of returning, authenticated customers; the CRM stays the system of record | Announced, no date |
| Routines | Automations before, during and after calls, such as follow-ups, reminders, waitlist offers or CRM writes | Announced, no date |
| ElevenAI | Workplace app that takes meeting notes and action items, with consent features | Free early access |
| Partner program | 550+ partners in 44 countries, per the company | Launched |
| Marketplace | Industry agents from partners for healthcare, finance, insurance, education and retail | Purchasable from next year |

### Custom models

The headline technical change is that businesses will be able to tune models on data from their own deployments and pick the metric they care about, such as customer satisfaction or reliability. CEO Mati Staniszewski likened this to giving each company its own reinforcement-learning setup. TNW reported that the work will happen with ElevenLabs researchers over the coming months; no pricing was given.

### Memory and routines

Memory lets an agent recall details about a returning customer, for example a preferred language or the times they can talk. Routines cover multi-step work around a call: pre-screening a mortgage applicant, calling a patient with a reminder, or offering a freed-up slot to someone on a waitlist.

### Partners and marketplace

Named partners include Google Cloud, IBM, Deloitte, DXC and Genesys, and ElevenLabs announced an alliance with McKinsey. Partners either build integrations or work alongside ElevenLabs' forward-deployed engineers. The marketplace lists industry-specific agents from technology partners; ElevenLabs says customers can buy them with their existing ElevenLabs commitment starting next year.

The company also expanded its partnership with NVIDIA at the summit, making Nemotron 3.5 Super VL available inside ElevenAgents and adding compute capacity for large deployments.

## Why it matters for teams that sell or support by phone

Three of these features target problems anyone running AI calls will recognise:

- **Memory.** Agents that treat every caller as a stranger frustrate repeat customers. Recall of preferences and history is quickly becoming a baseline expectation, though it raises data-handling questions you should settle before switching it on.
- **Routines.** Most of the value in a sales call is in what happens after it: the booking, the follow-up, the CRM update. Moving that work into the agent platform reduces the glue code teams write themselves.
- **Per-task model routing.** Using a cheap model for simple turns and a stronger one for hard ones is a sensible way to control cost and [latency](/blog/ai-voice-agent-latency-and-natural-conversation).

Fine-tuning on live data is the most ambitious item, and the least proven. It needs a large volume of calls to work, so it is aimed at big deployments for now.
