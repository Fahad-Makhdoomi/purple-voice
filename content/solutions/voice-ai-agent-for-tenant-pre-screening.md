Tenant pre-screening calls are the quick conversation between a rental enquiry and a viewing: is the move-in date realistic, is the rent within reach, how many people, any pets. On a busy listing, property managers can spend whole afternoons on those calls and still lose enquirers who wanted an answer in minutes. An AI voice agent takes that first call, books viewings for fitting enquirers and leaves referencing and the decision to you.

## What an AI voice agent does for tenant enquiries

- **Answers the listing questions.** Rent, deposit, bills, parking, floor, furnishings and pet policy come from your knowledge base.
- **Checks the basics.** Earliest move-in date, tenancy length, number of occupants and whether the monthly rent is within the budget they have in mind.
- **Books viewings.** Real slots on Google Calendar, so there is no back-and-forth over times.
- **Reaches out first.** For enquiries left on a portal form, a campaign can call them while the listing is still fresh in mind.
- **Works out of hours.** Evening and weekend callers get an answer, which is when many renters browse.
- **Leaves a record.** Transcript and summary show what was asked and what was promised, handy when two people remember a conversation differently.

## Viewing demand, sorted

| Enquirer | What the agent does | Your next step |
|---|---|---|
| Budget and dates fit | Offers a viewing slot | Attend viewing |
| Dates do not line up | Notes the preferred date, offers to flag future vacancies | Decide whether to follow up |
| Wants a pet-friendly unit and the listing is not | States the policy, closes politely | None |
| Asks about something not in the knowledge base | Says it will pass the question on | Reply by phone or message |
| Mentions urgency or an unusual situation | Records it, offers a human callback | Review personally |

## A sample call

*Illustrative only.*

**Agent:** Hello, this is an AI assistant for the letting team. You enquired about the two-bedroom flat near the park. Is now a good time?

**Caller:** Yes, I was hoping to move next month.

**Agent:** It is free from the first of next month. The rent is as listed and the deposit is five weeks. Would that work for you?

**Caller:** That's in range. Is it okay for a small dog?

**Agent:** Pets are considered case by case. I will note a small dog, and the manager can confirm at the viewing. How many people would be moving in?

**Caller:** Two adults.

**Agent:** Thanks. I can offer Thursday at five or Saturday at ten.

**Caller:** Saturday.

**Agent:** Booked for Saturday at ten. You will get the address and details by message.

## What to set up first

1. **Write the listing sheet.** Rent, deposit, bills, availability, minimum term, pet and smoking rules and the viewing process.
2. **Choose what to ask.** Dates, occupants, budget range and viewing availability. Nothing about family situation, origin or similar.
3. **Set the agent prompt.** Say clearly what the agent may promise (a viewing) and what it may not (approval).
4. **Connect Google Calendar** with the viewing windows you can actually staff.
5. **Configure campaign hours and retries** for portal leads, up to five attempts at spaced times.
6. **Turn on consent confirmation** and AI disclosure.
7. **Test with your own phone,** including a caller who asks about a pet or a discount.

## Where it falls short

- It does not run credit or reference checks, and it should never imply an applicant is approved.
- It cannot read a room. Some enquirers want reassurance from a person, and a handoff option should always exist.
- Fair housing and tenant-screening laws differ by place. This is general information, not legal advice; check current rules before you set criteria.
- Stale listing information leads to wrong answers, so update the knowledge base when a unit is let.
