AI interview scheduling calls remove the slowest part of hiring: the gap between deciding to interview someone and actually having a time in the diary. The agent phones the candidate, reads availability from Google Calendar, offers real slots and confirms the booking before the call ends. It is for recruiters and hiring managers who lose days to missed calls, long email threads and candidates who answer a day late.

## Why scheduling stalls

Scheduling looks trivial and is not. A recruiter proposes three slots by message. The candidate replies hours later, after one has been taken. A second round of slots goes out. By the time the interview is booked, a week has passed and the candidate has had another interview, or lost interest. Multiply this across a dozen open roles and several interviewers, and scheduling becomes a part-time job.

A phone call collapses the loop. If the candidate agrees to a slot while on the line, the booking is done. The agent can only offer times that are actually free, which means no double bookings.

## What an AI voice agent does for interview scheduling

- **Offers real slots.** It reads the connected Google Calendar and proposes only times that are available.
- **Confirms by repeating back.** Day, time, format and location or video link are read out so errors are caught on the call.
- **Answers the logistics questions.** How long will it take, who will I meet, where do I park, what should I bring. These come from your knowledge base.
- **Handles reschedules.** A candidate who calls back to move the slot can be served without a recruiter.
- **Retries missed calls.** Up to 5 attempts at set retry hours, rather than a single voicemail.
- **Writes the summary for the interviewer.** Preferences, concerns raised and notice period are captured for whoever runs the interview.
- **Passes complications to a person.** Accessibility needs, travel from abroad or a request for a different format are flagged for follow-up.

## Call type, response, handoff

| Situation | What the agent does | When it hands off |
|---|---|---|
| First scheduling call | Offers three slots, books one | Candidate wants a time that is not available |
| Candidate calls back to reschedule | Offers alternatives | No suitable slot exists |
| Candidate has a question about the role | Answers from knowledge base | Question is about pay negotiation or contract terms |
| Candidate needs adjustments | Records the request | Always passes to a recruiter |
| Interview panel changes | Not automatic | Recruiter updates the calendar |

## A sample call

Illustrative.

**Agent:** Hello, this is an AI assistant from the recruitment team. You were shortlisted for the operations coordinator role. Are you free to arrange an interview?
**Candidate:** Yes, that is good news.
**Agent:** The interview is forty-five minutes on video with the hiring manager. I have Tuesday at 11 am, Wednesday at 3 pm or Friday at 9 am. Which works best?
**Candidate:** Wednesday at 3.
**Agent:** Wednesday at 3 pm, forty-five minutes on video. Is the email address we have for you still right for the link?
**Candidate:** Yes.
**Agent:** All set. A reminder call will come the day before. Is there anything you need us to know ahead of time?
**Candidate:** No, thank you.

## What to set up first

1. **Connect Google Calendar** and check which calendar the agent reads. A single shared interview calendar is simplest.
2. **Set buffer and working hours** in the calendar so slots are not offered at awkward times.
3. **Write the interview facts** into the knowledge base: format, duration, interviewer role, location, preparation.
4. **Decide on reminders.** A confirmation call the day before can sit in the same flow. See [reducing candidate no-shows](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-reducing-candidate-no-shows).
5. **Set campaign hours and retries,** up to 5, to suit when candidates actually pick up.
6. **Enable consent confirmation and AI disclosure.**
7. **Test-call your own phone** and check that the booking appears under Appointments.

## Where it falls short

Scheduling works well when availability is clear. It struggles when an interview needs several panellists with conflicting diaries, or when each candidate needs a different format. The agent can book, but it cannot negotiate between interviewers. It may mishear email addresses, so use it to confirm a booking rather than to collect new contact details when accuracy matters. And a booked interview is not an attended one; pair it with reminders.
