An AI voice agent for ecommerce support answers the calls an online store gets about delivery, returns and products, and passes refunds, complaints and unusual cases to a person. It is for store owners and support leads who want a phone line that is always answered without hiring for it.

Phone is a small channel for most online shops, but the callers matter. They are often about to buy something expensive, or they have a problem and their patience is thin. Both deserve a fast, clear answer.

## What an AI voice agent does for ecommerce support

- **Explains returns.** Time window, condition rules, who pays postage and how to start the return.
- **Answers delivery questions.** Shipping options, typical times, regions served and what happens when a parcel is late.
- **Helps before the sale.** Sizing, materials, compatibility, stock timing and care instructions from the knowledge base.
- **Takes order details.** Order number and reason for calling, handed to your team in the summary.
- **Follows up abandoned carts.** A short outbound call to people who consented, offering help.
- **Escalates properly.** Refunds, damaged goods and complaints go to a human with notes.

## Call type and handling

| Call type | What the agent does | Hand-off |
|---|---|---|
| "How do I return this?" | Explains policy and steps | Person if outside the window |
| "Will this fit me?" | Answers from the size guide | Person for unusual fit questions |
| "My parcel is late" | Acknowledges, notes the order, offers callback | Person for the remedy |
| "I received the wrong item" | Takes details | Person decides replacement or refund |
| "Is this in stock?" | States what the knowledge base says | Person if live stock is needed |

## A sample call

Illustrative script; no real shop.

Caller: Hi, I want to send back a jacket I bought last week.

Agent: I can help with that. Our returns window is thirty days from delivery, and items need to be unworn with tags attached. Is that the case here?

Caller: Yes, I just tried it on and it is too big.

Agent: Then you are covered. To start a return, use the link in your order confirmation, or I can pass your details to the team to send you a return label. Which do you prefer?

Caller: Send me the label.

Agent: I will note that and the team will send it. Can I take your order number and the best contact number?

The agent applied the policy, did not issue anything itself, and left a clean record for the team.

## What to set up first

1. **Collect returns and delivery wording.** Use your own policy pages, rewritten for the ear.
2. **Add product facts.** Size guides, materials, care and compatibility. Update them with each range change.
3. **Set limits in the prompt.** State that the agent may explain policy but not approve exceptions or refunds.
4. **Set the handoff.** Warm transfer for complaints and high-value orders, with business-hours rules. See [warm transfer](/blog/warm-transfer-ai-to-human).
5. **Optional: abandoned-cart campaign.** Upload consented contacts, choose calling hours and retries, and keep the script short.
6. **Test from your own phone.** Try a return that is out of policy and see what the agent does.
7. **Add an AI disclosure.** One sentence at the start of every call.

## Where it falls short

The agent cannot see live stock or a customer's order unless your team builds that link through webhooks or the API on the Pro plan. Without it, it answers from policy and notes the rest.

It should not decide a refund, waive a fee or negotiate. It will also not replace the chat or email channels that most shoppers prefer, and it cannot send a return label or tracking message by itself, because email and WhatsApp sending are not features of Purple Voice today.

Abandoned-cart calling is a sensitive tactic. It can irritate people who did not expect it, so use consent, keep it brief and measure opt-outs before scaling.
