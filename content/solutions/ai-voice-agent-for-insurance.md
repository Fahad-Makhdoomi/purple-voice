An AI voice agent for insurance is a phone agent that holds a real conversation with people about quotes, renewals and claims, collects what your team needs and passes anything regulated to a licensed person. It suits agencies, brokers and small carriers where the same dozen call types arrive all day and the first few minutes of each follow a pattern.

Insurance phone work splits cleanly into two kinds. Some of it is information gathering and scheduling, which is repetitive and time-sensitive. The rest is advice, which needs judgement and a licence. The agent takes the first kind so your people can spend their hours on the second.

## What an AI voice agent does for insurance teams

- **Answers quote requests fast.** A person who asked for a quote is usually comparing three or four providers. The agent calls back while the request is fresh, confirms the basics and books a call with a producer. See [auto insurance leads](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-auto-insurance-leads) for the detail.
- **Chases renewals.** It calls policyholders ahead of the renewal date, checks whether anything has changed and books a review slot. More on that in [policy renewal calls](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-policy-renewals).
- **Takes first notice of loss.** It captures the facts of an incident in a structured way and routes urgent cases. The [claims intake page](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-claims-intake) covers the script.
- **Follows up abandoned quotes.** Quotes that stalled at the price or payment step get a polite check-in. See [quote follow-up](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-insurance-quote-follow-up).
- **Covers evenings and weekends.** Inbound calls get answered instead of rolling to voicemail. The [after-hours page](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-insurance-after-hours) explains the setup.
- **Revives lapsed policies and finds cross-sell moments.** Both are list-based campaigns: [win-back](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-lapsed-policy-winback) and [cross-sell](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-insurance-cross-sell).
- **Writes everything down.** Each call produces a transcript and an AI summary, so no one has to rely on a half-remembered phone conversation.

## Where the calls go

| Call type | What the agent does | When it hands off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New quote request | Confirms contact details and the type of cover wanted, books a callback | The caller asks which policy to choose |
| Renewal reminder | States the renewal date, asks if circumstances changed, offers a review slot | The caller wants to change cover or dispute the price |
| First notice of loss | Records who, what, when and where, plus injuries and safety | Injury, a disputed fault or a total-loss question |
| Abandoned quote | Asks what stopped them, answers factual questions from the knowledge base | They are ready to buy or want advice |
| After-hours inbound | Captures the reason for the call and urgency | An emergency, or anything needing a licensed answer |

## A sample call

Illustrative only, not a real recording.

**Agent:** Hello, this is the automated assistant for a local insurance agency. You asked for a home insurance quote earlier today. Is now an okay time for two quick questions?
**Caller:** Sure.
**Agent:** Thanks. Is the property you want covered your main home, and roughly when was it built?
**Caller:** Main home, built in the nineties.
**Agent:** Got it. Would you like a licensed adviser to go through the options with you? I can book a call tomorrow morning or afternoon.
**Caller:** Morning is good. Will it cost anything?
**Agent:** I do not have pricing to quote, so the adviser will give you figures on the call. I have you down for tomorrow at ten. You will get a confirmation shortly.

## What to set up first

1. **Write the agent prompt** with the products you write, a plain statement that the agent is an AI, and a list of topics it must refer on, such as price, exclusions and recommendations.
2. **Load the knowledge base** with office hours, how a claim is reported, what documents a renewal needs and where to find forms.
3. **Connect Google Calendar** so appointments land on the right producer's diary.
4. **Set campaign hours and retries.** Insurance leads are often reached in the evening. Allow up to five retries across different time slots.
5. **Turn on warm transfer** to a licensed person for ready-to-buy callers.
6. **Add the consent confirmation** step where your rules require it, and record the disclosure in the opening line.
7. **Test on your own phone** using the agent's test-call button before any real list is dialled. Read the transcript after.

## Where it falls short

The agent does not advise. It will not tell someone whether they are underinsured, interpret an exclusion or promise that a claim will be paid. It can mishear policy numbers and unusual surnames, so confirm key identifiers back to the caller and let humans verify before anything is changed on a policy. Distressed callers, such as someone ringing after a serious accident, should reach a person quickly. Build that route first and treat it as non-negotiable.

Rules for outbound calling and for insurance sales conduct vary by country and state. This is general information, not legal advice. Read [TCPA and AI voice calls](/blog/tcpa-and-ai-voice-calls) and [disclosing AI on calls](/blog/disclose-ai-on-calls), and check the current rules for your market.
