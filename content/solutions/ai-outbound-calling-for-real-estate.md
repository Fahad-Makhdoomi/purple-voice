AI outbound calling for real estate works best on the calls that follow a clear signal: a portal enquiry, a viewing request or a lapsed listing where the owner may be open to talking. The agent makes the first, short call; you or your agents handle everyone who shows interest. Used on cold property lists it carries more legal and reputational risk, so this page starts with the safe lists and ends with the compliance questions. The general system is described in the [AI outbound calling system overview](/solutions/ai-outbound-calling-system).

## Which real estate calls an agent can make

- **Portal and website enquiries.** Phone within minutes, confirm what they asked about and offer a viewing.
- **Viewing confirmations.** Remind buyers or tenants the day before and offer to reschedule. See [appointment reminder calls](/solutions/ai-outbound-calling-for-appointment-reminders).
- **Valuation requests.** Ask when the owner wants to sell and book a valuation visit.
- **Expired or withdrawn listings.** Check whether the owner still wants to sell, subject to the rules in your area.
- **FSBO owners.** Ask whether they would take a short conversation about support, again subject to local rules.
- **Old buyer databases.** Check whether searches are still active and update preferences. See [reactivating old leads with AI calls](/blog/reactivate-old-leads-with-ai-calls).
- **Landlord and tenant follow-ups.** Confirm contact details and maintenance availability.

## Warm lists versus cold lists

| List | Typical basis | Risk | Suggested approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portal enquiry | They asked for contact | Low | Call fast, short script |
| Viewing booked | Existing relationship | Low | Reminder and confirm |
| Past clients | Prior relationship | Low to medium | Gentle check-in, easy opt-out |
| Expired listings | Public data, no consent | Medium to high | Check do-not-call and local rules first |
| FSBO owners | Public data, no consent | Medium to high | Check rules, be transparent, call sparingly |

## A sample call

Illustrative, for a lapsed listing where local rules have been checked and the number is not on a do-not-call list.

**Agent:** Hello, is this Mr Okafor? I am an AI assistant calling for a local estate agency. I understand your listing ended last month. Is it all right if I ask one question?

**Caller:** Go on.

**Agent:** Are you still thinking of selling, or have you decided to stay?

**Caller:** We are still thinking about it, just not sure about price.

**Agent:** A valuation visit is free and has no obligation. Would you like one of the agents to ring you, or shall I book a visit?

**Caller:** Have them ring me tomorrow.

**Agent:** I will pass that on. Is the morning or afternoon better?

## What to set up first

1. **Pick the list with the clearest basis**, usually enquiries and booked viewings.
2. **Check do-not-call registers** and any rules on automated calls before using public-record lists.
3. **Write a short, honest opening** that says who is calling and why. The [disclosure guide](/blog/disclose-ai-on-calls) helps.
4. **Load listing data and area facts** into the knowledge base, and tell the agent to say "I will ask the agent" when unsure.
5. **Connect Google Calendar** for valuations and viewings, and add a warm transfer to a duty agent for hot enquiries.
6. **Set conservative retries.** Property owners dislike repeated calls, so consider 1 or 2 rather than the maximum of 5.
7. **Test on your own phone**, and ask the agent a price question to see whether it stays within its facts.

## Where AI outbound calling for real estate falls short

- **It does not know the local market on its own.** Anything about prices, trends or neighbourhoods must come from your knowledge base, and valuation opinions belong to a person.
- **Cold property calls can annoy.** Even where legal, repeated unsolicited calls can damage your name. Use few retries.
- **Rules differ widely.** TCPA-style consent requirements, do-not-call lists and automated-voice restrictions vary by country and state. This is general information, not legal advice, so check current rules, then read the [compliance overview](/solutions/ai-outbound-calling-compliance) and [TCPA and AI voice calls](/blog/tcpa-and-ai-voice-calls).
- **Negotiation and trust are human work.** Listing presentations, offers and difficult sellers stay with your agents.
- **Caller ID counts.** A local number is answered more often than an unknown one, covered in [caller ID and number reputation](/solutions/ai-outbound-calling-caller-id-and-number-reputation).
