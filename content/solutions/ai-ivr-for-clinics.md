AI IVR for clinics means an agent that answers the front-desk phone for administrative calls: booking and moving appointments, opening hours, directions and preparation instructions. It is meant for practices where reception is often busy with patients in front of them, and the phone competes for the same pair of hands. It is not a clinician, and it should never give medical advice.

That boundary is the design principle. Everything routine is automated; everything clinical, urgent or sensitive goes to your staff.

## What the agent takes off reception

- **New appointment requests.** The agent offers open slots and books them on Google Calendar.
- **Rescheduling and cancellations.** Callers move a slot without waiting on hold.
- **Opening hours and holiday closures.** Always current, if you keep the knowledge base current.
- **Directions and parking.** Entrances, lifts, bus routes, which door to use.
- **Preparation instructions.** "Do I need to bring anything?" answered from text you wrote and approved.
- **Forms and first-visit questions.** What to bring, how long to allow.
- **Message taking.** Requests for a clinician callback are recorded and summarised.

## Call type, agent action, hand-off

| Call | Agent does | Hands off to a person |
|---|---|---|
| Book check-up | Offers slots, books, confirms details | Only if no suitable slot |
| Reschedule | Finds the booking, offers new times | If the booking cannot be found |
| Where are you? | Gives directions and parking | Never |
| Test results or symptoms | Does not discuss | Always, or takes a callback request |
| Emergency described | Tells caller to contact emergency services | Immediately, per your rule |
| Billing query | Takes details | To the office, in hours |

## A sample call

Illustrative, for a physiotherapy clinic.

Agent: Good morning, you've reached the clinic's booking line. How can I help?
Caller: I'd like to see someone about my shoulder. Is Wednesday free?
Agent: I can book appointments, though I can't give medical advice. I have Wednesday at 3:40 with one of our physiotherapists. Would that work?
Caller: Yes. Is parking easy?
Agent: There's a pay-and-display car park behind the building, and the entrance is the blue door on Mill Lane. May I take your name and number for the booking?
Caller: It's Jamie Okafor...
Agent: Thank you, Jamie. Booked for Wednesday at 3:40. If the pain gets worse before then, please contact the clinic or your doctor.

## What to set up first for an AI IVR for clinics

1. **Write the boundary into the prompt.** "Handle administrative calls only. Do not discuss symptoms, diagnoses, medication or results."
2. **Add an emergency rule.** Describe what counts and what the agent says, then test it repeatedly.
3. **Fill the knowledge base** with hours, services, prices, directions, cancellation policy and first-visit guidance.
4. **Connect Google Calendar** for the right calendars, with buffer times and slots you do not want offered.
5. **Choose the transfer targets.** Who takes a clinical callback during opening hours, and what happens when no one is free.
6. **Add recording and disclosure wording.** Read [AI IVR recording and consent](/solutions/ai-ivr-compliance-and-recording).
7. **Test with reception and a clinician** before a single patient hears it.

## Where it falls short in a clinical setting

Health information is regulated and the rules vary by country and by service, so ask a qualified adviser what you may record and store; this page is general information only. The agent can mishear names, dates of birth or drug names, so keep it away from detailed clinical data. Patients who are anxious, elderly or in pain may want a person at once, and the transfer should be quick and unquestioned. If your appointment system is not Google Calendar, the booking step may need a manual confirmation from reception.
