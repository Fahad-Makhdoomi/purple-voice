Ai webinar follow up calls reach the people who registered for or attended your session while it is still fresh, ask what they thought and book a conversation with those who are interested. An AI voice agent makes these calls across the whole list in a day or two, something a small team rarely manages by hand. It suits marketing and sales teams running regular webinars or events.

## What AI webinar follow up calls do

- **Segment-specific calls.** Attendees, people who left early and no-shows each hear a different opener.
- **Quick feedback.** One open question about the topic produces useful comments for your content team.
- **Interest sorting.** Answers tell a rep who wants a conversation and who only wanted the slides.
- **Booking.** Interested people get a slot on Google Calendar.
- **Recording and resource sharing.** The agent can say a recording exists and note a request to be sent it, for your team to action.
- **Event-day context.** Prompt variables such as the session title keep the call relevant.
- **Reporting.** Summaries show common themes and the not-reached analytics show who needs another channel.

## One list, three scripts

| Segment | Opening angle | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Attended full session | "What did you think of the section on X?" | Book a call if the topic matters now |
| Left early | "Was there something that was not relevant?" | Learn why, then offer a short call |
| Registered, did not attend | "Sorry we missed you, the recording is available" | Ask if the topic is still live |

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

> **Agent:** Hello, this is the assistant calling on behalf of a payments platform. You attended our webinar on cross-border payouts yesterday.
> **Caller:** Yes, I joined for most of it.
> **Agent:** Thanks for coming. What stood out most?
> **Caller:** The part about reconciliation. That is our headache.
> **Agent:** That is a common one. Are you looking at changing how you handle it this year?
> **Caller:** Probably, yes.
> **Agent:** A specialist can walk through your setup in 20 minutes. Would Tuesday or Thursday work?
> **Caller:** Thursday.
> **Agent:** Booked. I will pass on that reconciliation is the main concern.

The open question gave the rep a specific angle, which is better than a generic "following up on the webinar".

## What to set up first

1. **Export the lists by segment** with fields for the session title and attendance status.
2. **Write three short openers,** one per segment, and keep each under 20 seconds.
3. **Build a knowledge base** with the webinar summary, recording link policy and a few common follow-up questions.
4. **Time the campaign** for the same or next working day, inside normal calling hours.
5. **Connect Google Calendar** for the reps who take meetings.
6. **Set retries up to 5,** spaced over a few days, but stop calling anyone who says they are not interested.
7. **Check how registrants consented** to be contacted, and enable consent confirmation where you need it.
8. **Place test calls on your own phone** to check the opener sounds natural.

## Making ai webinar follow up calls worth taking

People are more receptive when the call is about something they just watched. Keep the pitch out of the first half of the call and take any answer, including a no, as data. Over time, the summaries tell you which topics produce meetings and which only produce views.

## Where it falls short

- **Weak attendance data.** If your platform does not tell you who stayed, segments blur.
- **Hosts' content nuance.** The agent will not remember a speaker's exact point beyond what you wrote into the prompt.
- **Consent.** A webinar registration does not always cover a phone call. Check your form wording and current rules; this is general information, not legal advice.
- **Over-calling.** One call and a few retries is plenty; more feels like pressure.
- **High-value attendees.** Known target accounts deserve a human call, not an automated one.
