To qualify inbound leads with AI, you let a voice agent answer every incoming call and phone every form submission quickly, ask a few questions, and pass on the ones worth a conversation. It is for teams whose leads arrive while the sales staff are on other calls, in meetings or asleep. The overall method is in [AI call qualification](/solutions/ai-call-qualification); this page covers the inbound side.

## What an AI voice agent does for inbound leads

- **Answers live calls.** No voicemail, no ringing out. The agent greets the caller and starts qualifying or answering.
- **Calls form fills.** New submissions go into a campaign, and the agent phones them within your calling hours.
- **Retries when nobody answers.** Up to 5 retries with retry hours you set, so a missed call is not the end of the lead.
- **Answers common questions.** Prices, opening hours, areas covered and next steps come from the knowledge base.
- **Books or transfers.** Strong leads are warm-transferred while still on the line or booked into Google Calendar.
- **Leaves a record.** Transcripts, summaries and recordings are in the Calls section for the team to read.

## Two kinds of inbound, two set-ups

| Lead source | What the agent does | When it hands off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live inbound call | Greets, identifies the need, qualifies, answers questions | Caller asks for a person, or qualifies as hot |
| Web form submission | Phones the lead, references the enquiry, qualifies | Lead qualifies, or asks to speak to someone |
| Missed call from earlier | Calls back with an apology and one question | Lead is interested, or asks for a human |

A form lead did ask to hear from you, so the agent can open by referring to what they submitted. That is a warmer start than a cold call and usually gets more people to stay on the line.

## A sample call

*Illustrative only; no real brand names.*

**Agent:** Hello, this is the assistant for a local accountancy firm. You filled in a form earlier about tax return help. Is this a good time?
**Caller:** Yes, I was just about to ring you.
**Agent:** Great timing. Is it for yourself or a business?
**Caller:** I'm self-employed, so it's me.
**Agent:** And is it for this tax year, or are you catching up on earlier ones?
**Caller:** This year and last year, I missed it.
**Agent:** Thanks, I'll note that. An accountant can talk you through it. Would tomorrow at ten work for a call?
**Caller:** Yes, please.

## Why inbound deserves its own set-up

Inbound callers are further along than a cold list. They are asking you for something, so the agent should skip the pitch, confirm what they want and get to qualification fast. This also changes the failure mode: a cold lead who hangs up is a shrug, but an inbound caller who is kept waiting or lost is a lead you paid to attract. See [speed to lead with AI calling](/blog/speed-to-lead-ai-calling) for more on why first-minute response matters.

## What to set up first

1. Buy or connect a phone number you control and route inbound calls to the agent.
2. Write the agent prompt: greeting, three to five qualification questions, and the transfer rules.
3. Add your services, prices, areas and opening hours to the knowledge base.
4. Create a campaign for form fills with calling hours and retry hours (up to 5 retries).
5. Connect Google Calendar so qualified leads can book without a person in the middle.
6. Choose a transfer number for hot calls and set what the agent says before it connects them.
7. Test-call your own phone as a caller, and again as a form lead.

A [launch checklist](/resources/ai-voice-agent-launch-checklist) is useful for the final pass.

## Where inbound qualification falls short

- Some callers want a person immediately. Make the human route obvious, or you will annoy them.
- Form data can be wrong. Wrong numbers and fake names will not be fixed by the agent.
- Complex or emotional enquiries, such as complaints, belong with a person.
- Calling a form lead may depend on what the form said about contact. Check current rules and the wording of your consent text.
- The agent only knows what you give it. If the knowledge base is thin, callers will hit dead ends.

## Measuring whether inbound qualification is working

Track four numbers each week: calls answered, form leads reached, qualified leads and booked appointments. Compare answered-by-agent with your old answered-by-person rate, because the gain is usually in the calls you used to miss. Then read ten transcripts: callers who hung up early show where the opening is weak, and callers who asked for a human show where your qualification feels like an obstacle. If many callers ask a question the agent cannot answer, add it to the knowledge base the same day. After a month you will know which source, such as ads, referrals or directory listings, produces callers who qualify, and that is information your marketing spend can use.
