AI missed call follow up means two things working together: an agent that answers calls your team could not take, and an agent that rings back the numbers that slipped through anyway. It is for trades, clinics, salons, agencies and any small team that cannot staff every minute of the phone.

A missed call does not always become a lost customer, but it often does. Many callers try one other business before they try you again.

## What AI missed call follow up does

- **Answers overflow.** When the line is busy or nobody picks up, the agent answers instead.
- **Captures the reason.** Name, number, what they need and how urgent it is.
- **Books the next step.** An appointment or a human callback slot on Google Calendar.
- **Rings back unanswered calls.** An outbound campaign from your missed-number list.
- **Retries up to five times.** With retry hours that you set, and a not-reached view for the rest.
- **Summarises everything.** Each call has an AI summary, a transcript and a recording.

## Which kind of missed call, which response

| Missed call type | Best response | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Busy line during the day | Agent answers overflow | Person for complex cases |
| Call outside opening hours | Agent answers, books callback | Next-morning review |
| Unanswered, no voicemail | Outbound ring back | Person if the caller wants one |
| Repeat caller, upset | Transfer at once | Person always |
| Wrong number or spam | Agent politely ends | None |

## A sample call

Illustrative only; no real business.

Agent: Hello, this is an AI assistant for the workshop. We missed your call earlier. Is this Jordan?

Caller: Yes. I called about a leak under my sink.

Agent: Thank you for calling back. I can book an engineer. Is the water still running, or is it a slow drip?

Caller: Slow drip, but it is getting worse.

Agent: Understood. I have a slot tomorrow at nine. Shall I book that?

Caller: Yes please.

Agent: Booked for tomorrow at nine. If it turns into a flood before then, call this number and choose to speak to the team. Is the address the same as the one on the call?

## What to set up first

1. **Measure the gaps.** Count missed calls by hour so you know whether you need overflow answering, ring back or both.
2. **Choose the routing.** Forward busy and unanswered calls to the agent, or run it on a dedicated number.
3. **Write urgency rules.** What triggers a transfer, and to whom.
4. **Connect Google Calendar.** So the agent books real slots.
5. **Prepare the ring-back list.** Numbers and times, with the reason if you know it.
6. **Confirm consent.** The campaign asks for it. See [AI calling compliance basics](/resources/sales-call-compliance-checklist).
7. **Test from your own phone.** Cover a busy-line scenario, a ring-back and an angry caller.

## Where it falls short

If a missed call was spam, a wrong number or a caller who wanted a person and no one else, the follow-up adds no value. The agent cannot judge a caller's mood as well as a person, and it should transfer quickly when someone sounds upset.

It also cannot reach people who do not answer unknown numbers. Retries help but are not a guarantee, and repeated calls to people who ignore them can be annoying, so keep the retry count modest.

The agent does not create demand. It saves what was already ringing. For general-purpose phone coverage, it works best alongside a team that acts on the summaries.
