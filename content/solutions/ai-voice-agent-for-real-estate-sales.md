An ai voice agent for real estate sales answers property enquiries and calls new leads while your agents are out, asks the questions that sort serious buyers and sellers from browsers and books viewings or valuations on Google Calendar. It is for agency owners and agents who lose leads because nobody can pick up during a showing.

## What an AI voice agent does for real estate sales

- **Answers listing enquiries.** Callers get an answer about availability, area and viewing arrangements without waiting for a callback.
- **Qualifies buyers.** Budget range, timeline, financing status and areas of interest.
- **Qualifies sellers.** Property type, timing and what prompted the sale.
- **Books viewings.** It offers slots and creates the event, including the address you configure.
- **Books valuations** with the right agent, using a separate seller prompt.
- **Reconfirms before viewings** with a short reminder call.
- **Logs everything.** Transcripts and summaries become the lead notes.

For a deeper look at the agent workflow, see the [guide to AI calling for real estate agents](/blog/ai-calling-for-real-estate-agents).

## Buyers and sellers need different calls

| | Buyer enquiry | Seller lead |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Book a viewing | Book a valuation |
| Key questions | Budget, timeline, financing, must-haves | Property type, timing, reason for selling |
| Agent hands off when | Offer is mentioned or caller wants negotiation | Caller asks about price or commission |
| Follow-up | Reminder call before viewing | Reminder and confirmation of access |

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

> **Agent:** Hello, this is the assistant for a residential sales agency. You enquired about the three-bedroom on the park road listing.
> **Caller:** Yes, is it still available?
> **Agent:** I can see it is still listed. Are you looking to move soon?
> **Caller:** Within three months. We are selling our flat first.
> **Agent:** Thanks. Do you have a budget range in mind?
> **Caller:** Up to the listed price, maybe a bit under.
> **Agent:** I cannot discuss price movement, but the agent can. I can book a viewing on Saturday at 11 or 12:30.
> **Caller:** 11 is fine.
> **Agent:** Booked for Saturday at 11. I will call on Friday to confirm.

The agent stayed away from negotiation but captured the key fact: the caller needs to sell first.

## What to set up first

1. **Pick the first use case.** New portal enquiries are the easiest to start with.
2. **Write two prompts,** one for buyers and one for sellers, with separate qualifying questions.
3. **Add a knowledge base** with the areas you cover, how viewings work, and what the agent should not say about prices, offers or neighbourhood claims.
4. **Connect Google Calendar** for each agent who runs viewings.
5. **Set calling hours** that avoid early mornings and late evenings, and up to 5 retries for unanswered enquiries.
6. **Test on your own phone** by asking about a listing the agent does not know.
7. **Decide the transfer rule** for callers who are ready to make an offer.

## Using an ai voice agent for real estate sales sensibly

Property is local and personal. Keep the agent to scheduling and basic qualification, and make sure every summary reaches a named agent quickly. Old enquiry lists are also a good place to start; see [reactivating old leads with AI calls](/blog/reactivate-old-leads-with-ai-calls).

## Where it falls short

- **Pricing and valuation.** The agent should not offer opinions on value or negotiate.
- **Regulated claims.** Anything about schools, crime, investment returns or financing advice stays with licensed people.
- **Listing accuracy.** If the knowledge base is out of date, the agent will repeat it, so update listings regularly.
- **Cold calling owners or expired listings** is restricted in many places. Check current rules and registers; this is general information, not legal advice.
- **Emotion.** Selling a home can be stressful, and some sellers want a person from the first call.
