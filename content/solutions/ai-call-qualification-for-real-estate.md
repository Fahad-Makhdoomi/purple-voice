To qualify real estate leads with AI, you have a voice agent phone each new enquiry, ask a handful of questions about location, budget, finance and timing, and book viewings for buyers who look serious. It is for estate agents, team leaders and independent agents who get portal enquiries faster than they can return them. Wider method: [AI call qualification](/solutions/ai-call-qualification).

## What an AI voice agent does for real estate leads

- **Calls fresh enquiries while interest is high.** Someone who has just messaged about a listing is far more likely to talk now than tomorrow.
- **Separates buyers, sellers and renters.** The first question settles which script applies.
- **Checks readiness.** Budget range, deposit or pre-approval status, and whether they must sell first.
- **Captures search criteria.** Areas, bedrooms, property type and any must-haves.
- **Books viewings or valuations.** Slots come from your Google Calendar.
- **Hands hot leads to a negotiator.** A warm transfer connects the buyer while they are still engaged.
- **Chases the rest.** Retries up to 5 times with retry hours, and not-reached analytics show who never answered.

## Buyer, seller, renter: what each call checks

| Lead type | Key questions | Pass signal | Handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer | Area, budget, pre-approval, must sell first, move date | Realistic budget, finance clear, moving within 6 months | Viewing booked |
| Seller | Address, reason for selling, timeline, other agents | Wants a valuation soon | Valuation appointment |
| Renter | Move date, budget, pets, household size | Move within 4 to 6 weeks | Viewing or shortlist |
| Investor | Yield target, cash or mortgage, portfolio size | Active and decisive | Senior negotiator call |

Real estate leads often have a long tail of casual browsers. The agent keeps them in a lower bucket without throwing them away; a person can nurture them later, and [reactivating old leads with AI calls](/blog/reactivate-old-leads-with-ai-calls) is one way to do it.

## A sample call

*Illustrative only. Figures are made up for the example.*

**Agent:** Hello, I'm the assistant for a local estate agency. You enquired about the three-bedroom on the new listing. Are you still looking in that area?
**Caller:** Yes, we're keen.
**Agent:** Do you have a property to sell first?
**Caller:** We're renting at the moment.
**Agent:** Have you spoken to a lender about what you can borrow?
**Caller:** We have an agreement in principle for around two hundred and eighty thousand.
**Agent:** That's helpful. When would you like to move?
**Caller:** Within four months if possible.
**Agent:** Our negotiator has viewing slots on Thursday and Saturday. Which suits?

## What to set up first

1. Decide the buyer, seller and renter paths and write one short script for each.
2. Put the qualification questions into the agent prompt, including how to handle "I'm just looking".
3. Add listing basics, area information and viewing rules to the knowledge base, so the agent can answer simple property questions without inventing details.
4. Upload leads as a campaign, set calling hours that suit buyers (evenings and weekends help), and choose retry hours.
5. Connect Google Calendar with each negotiator's viewing slots.
6. Set a warm-transfer number for hot buyers who want to speak now.
7. Test-call your own phone as each lead type.

Read [AI calling for real estate agents](/blog/ai-calling-for-real-estate-agents) for how teams work with this day to day.

## Where it falls short

- It cannot confirm a mortgage agreement or proof of funds. Pre-approval is a claim.
- It does not give property, legal or mortgage advice. Keep answers to facts in your knowledge base and hand over anything else.
- Property details change quickly. A stale knowledge base produces wrong answers about availability or price.
- Sellers often want to meet the person who will market their home. The agent can book the valuation but should not try to win the instruction.
- Outreach rules vary by country and by how the lead consented. This is general information, not legal advice, so check current rules.

## Working the leads that do not qualify yet

In property, many people who are not ready today will be ready in a few months. Treat the lower bucket as a pipeline, not a bin. Ask the agent to record when the lead expects to move and what is holding them back, such as selling first or waiting on finance. Your team can then set a reminder call near that date, and a later campaign can re-contact them with an update on new listings or a changed market. Keep the wording honest and low pressure. A buyer who felt respected during an early "not yet" call is far more likely to take your call, and to book a viewing, when their situation changes.
