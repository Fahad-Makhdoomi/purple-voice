Insurance cross sell calls reach customers who hold one policy with you and may hold others elsewhere. An AI voice agent asks whether they have a particular cover, notes when it renews and books an adviser for those who would like a comparison. It suits agencies with a loyal base of single-product customers.

## Why a question beats a pitch

Customers do not like being sold to by a stranger, but they answer simple questions from a provider they know. "Do you have your home insurance with us?" is a fact-finding question. If the answer is no, you learn who covers them and when that renews. That date is more valuable than the call itself, because it tells you when to come back.

The agent is good at asking that question consistently across hundreds of customers. It is not good at reading hints in someone's tone, so the follow-up belongs to a person.

## What an AI voice agent does for cross-sell

- **Starts from the existing relationship.** "You have your auto cover with us."
- **Asks about one gap only.** Home, life, business or travel, not a menu.
- **Captures the other provider and renewal month**, when the customer is willing to share.
- **Respects "I am happy".** A clean exit and a note.
- **Books advisers** on Google Calendar for customers who want a comparison.
- **Flags life events.** New house, new baby or retirement mentioned in passing are logged.

## Customer type and approach

| Customer | Gap to ask about | Agent says | Hand off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Auto only | Home or renters | Asks if home is insured elsewhere | If they want a comparison |
| Home only | Auto | Same, with renewal date | If interested |
| Mortgage holder | Life cover | Asks if they have any, neutral | Always to adviser |
| Small business owner | Commercial | Checks who covers premises | Commercial adviser |
| Recent mover | Update and bundle | Confirms new address | Adviser for re-quote |

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

**Agent:** Hello, this is an automated assistant from your insurance agency. You have your car insurance with us, and I wanted to ask one question. Do you have your home insurance with us too?
**Caller:** No, that is with someone else.
**Agent:** Thanks. Do you know roughly when it renews?
**Caller:** March, I think.
**Agent:** Would you like an adviser to look at it nearer the time, in case a combined arrangement makes sense?
**Caller:** Sure, ring me in February.
**Agent:** I will note February. Thank you.

## What to set up first

1. **Define the gap per campaign.** One product per call list. Do not mix.
2. **Segment the list** by customer tenure so long-standing customers get a gentler opener.
3. **Write the prompt** with a firm rule against implying savings it cannot confirm.
4. **Add a knowledge base entry** describing what an adviser will review and what they will need.
5. **Set calling hours** and two retries at most. Existing customers should not feel chased.
6. **Use Google Calendar** for adviser slots, including future dates for renewal-timed callbacks.
7. **Test on your phone** and rehearse a customer who says "I am happy where I am".

## Where it falls short

Cross-selling invites the temptation to talk past what the customer needs. The agent should not guess their circumstances, claim a bundle is cheaper or suggest cover they may not require. It cannot see competitor policies or judge whether a gap really exists. Customers on an active claim or with a recent complaint should be removed from the list. Results depend on your product mix, and a modest lift in booked reviews is a realistic aim.
