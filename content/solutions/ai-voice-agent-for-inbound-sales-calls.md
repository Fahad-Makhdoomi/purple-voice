An ai voice agent for inbound sales calls answers your sales line every time it rings, finds out what the caller wants, answers common questions and then books a meeting or transfers the call. It is aimed at businesses where a missed enquiry is lost revenue and where reps are often on other calls, in meetings or off shift.

## What an AI voice agent does for inbound sales calls

- **Answers instantly.** No hold music, no voicemail, no ring-out at 6:01 pm.
- **Identifies the reason for the call.** New enquiry, existing customer, supplier or wrong number each get a different path.
- **Qualifies on the spot.** Size, timing, need and location are captured while the caller is engaged.
- **Answers from your knowledge base.** Opening hours, service areas, how pricing works, what is included.
- **Books a meeting.** It checks availability and writes to Google Calendar.
- **Transfers hot calls.** If someone is ready to buy, it can pass the live call to a person.
- **Logs the call.** A summary and transcript sit in the dashboard for whoever follows up.

## Call type and handling

| Caller says | Agent does | Hands off when |
|---|---|---|
| "I want a quote" | Collects scope and contact, offers a booking | Quote is custom or urgent |
| "What do you charge?" | Explains pricing structure from the knowledge base | Caller wants a bespoke figure |
| "Is [rep] there?" | Takes a message and offers a callback slot | Rep is available, if you set a transfer |
| "I am an existing customer" | Collects the issue and passes to the right team | Immediately, or by callback |
| "Wrong number" | Closes politely | Never |

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

> **Agent:** Good afternoon, you have reached a commercial cleaning company. I am the virtual assistant. How can I help?
> **Caller:** We need an office cleaned twice a week. What would that cost?
> **Agent:** Happy to help scope it. Roughly how big is the office, and which area is it in?
> **Caller:** About 3,000 square feet, near the city centre.
> **Agent:** We cover that area. The price depends on the layout and the hours, so a short site visit is the usual next step. Would Tuesday morning or Wednesday afternoon be better?
> **Caller:** Tuesday morning.
> **Agent:** Done, 10 am Tuesday. Can I confirm the best number to reach you?

The summary for the sales lead lists the size, location and visit time, so the team knows the quote can be prepared before they arrive.

## What to set up first

1. **Decide the number routing.** Point the line at the agent, and decide what a caller hears if they ask for a person at a time nobody is free.
2. **Write the greeting.** Short, clear about who is speaking, and ready to be interrupted.
3. **Add the knowledge base.** Service areas, what you do not do, how pricing works and the usual objections.
4. **List the qualifying questions,** no more than five or six, in the order a human would ask them.
5. **Connect Google Calendar** and set meeting length and buffers.
6. **Configure the transfer number** and write the conditions for using it.
7. **Run test calls from a mobile and a landline,** including a bad line.
8. **Check disclosure and recording wording** against current rules where you operate.

## Why an ai voice agent for inbound sales calls beats a missed call

A caller who reaches voicemail often tries the next supplier. A caller who reaches a helpful agent gets an answer and a booked next step. The point is not to be clever; it is to stop losing the call. Pair the agent with a clear human follow-up so what it books is honoured.

## Where it falls short

- **Complex enquiries.** Bespoke quotes, contract questions and negotiation go to a person.
- **Angry or distressed callers.** Route them to a human quickly.
- **Existing-customer problems.** This page is about sales; service issues need their own agent or process.
- **Unusual accents, poor lines and background noise** can lead to misheard details, so confirm names, numbers and addresses back to the caller.
- **Over-promising.** If the knowledge base is thin, the agent should say it will have someone follow up, and your prompt should force that.
