AI life insurance lead qualification means an AI voice agent calls people who asked about life cover, finds out whether and when they want to talk, and either books a licensed adviser or schedules another check-in. It is built for the long, patient shape of life leads, where a good prospect might need three or four calls before they pick a date.

## Life leads are slow, and that is the problem

A person looking at life cover is usually responding to something: a mortgage, a child, a change of job. The urgency is real but diffuse, and the first call often finds them in the middle of a day. Advisers either burn hours on cold callbacks or let the list decay. Compare the speed-led [auto lead](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-auto-insurance-leads) pattern: life rewards patience instead.

An agent can be patient at no extra cost per attempt, which is why this fits nurture campaigns so well.

## Life insurance call types

| Lead type | What the agent opens with | What it records | Hand off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh web enquiry | Why they looked, and whether now is a good time | Trigger event, timing | Ready to talk now |
| Older enquiry (30 to 90 days) | A gentle check on whether the need still exists | Still interested or not | Interested |
| Partner or family involved | Asks if the decision involves someone else | Names and best time to include them | Booking for both |
| "Just looking" | Offers to call back later | Preferred month | Not needed yet |
| Medical questions | Explains an adviser covers those | A flag for the adviser | Always |

## What an AI voice agent does for life insurance leads

- **Starts with a human question.** "What made you look at life cover?" is easier to answer than a form field.
- **Identifies the trigger.** New home, baby, business loan or a milestone birthday. The adviser opens with context.
- **Notes who else decides.** Partners often need to be on the call.
- **Respects timing.** Someone saying "after the summer" is scheduled into a later campaign, not chased again tomorrow.
- **Answers neutral questions.** What the adviser will cover, how long the call takes, what to have ready.
- **Books an adviser slot** on Google Calendar, with the summary attached.
- **Stops cleanly.** Anyone who declines is removed, not recontacted.

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

**Agent:** Hello, this is the automated assistant for a life insurance adviser. You asked about life cover a few weeks ago. Is now an alright time for a couple of questions?
**Caller:** I suppose, I was busy before.
**Agent:** No problem. What made you start looking?
**Caller:** We bought a house in the spring.
**Agent:** Thanks. Would your partner want to be part of a conversation with an adviser?
**Caller:** Yes, they would.
**Agent:** The adviser has an evening slot on Thursday. Shall I book it for both of you?
**Caller:** Thursday works.

## What to set up first

1. **Write a gentle, non-pushy prompt.** Short sentences, no urgency tricks, and a clear AI disclosure.
2. **Draw the line on health.** The agent must not ask underwriting questions or imply eligibility.
3. **Create two campaigns:** one for fresh leads and one for older leads with different openers.
4. **Set hours and up to five retries** over a couple of weeks, with different times of day.
5. **Book on Google Calendar**, including slots in the evening when couples are home.
6. **Use the summary field** to carry the trigger event and decision-makers into the adviser's notes.
7. **Test on your phone** and check how it deals with an emotional answer, such as a recent bereavement.

## Where it falls short

Life cover can be an emotional subject, and a bereaved or anxious caller needs a human. The agent can be set to stop and offer a person when it detects distress, but it will not always catch it. It cannot assess need, recommend a sum assured or discuss exclusions. Lead sources vary, and a list of people who clicked an ad and never wanted a call is a poor list regardless of the caller. Do not use the agent to apply pressure around mortality or fear.
