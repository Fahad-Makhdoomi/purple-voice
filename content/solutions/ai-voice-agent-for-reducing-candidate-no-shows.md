To reduce candidate no-shows, call the person shortly before the interview or start date, confirm the details, and make it easy to say they cannot come. An AI voice agent makes those calls at scale, asks the question directly and offers to reschedule on the spot. It is for recruiters whose diaries are full of slots that go empty and whose managers are tired of waiting at reception.

## What a no-show actually costs

A missed interview is more than twenty minutes of an empty chair. The hiring manager has cleared time. The recruiter has to chase and rebook. The role stays open for another week, and the next batch of candidates waits behind it. Missed first days are worse: induction, equipment and a place on a rota are all arranged for someone who does not arrive.

Many candidates do not turn up for ordinary reasons rather than bad faith. They forgot, they were offered something else, or something changed. Most of these cases are recoverable if somebody asks early enough, which is exactly what a confirmation call does.

## What an AI voice agent does to reduce candidate no-shows

- **Confirms attendance.** It states the day, time and place and asks whether the candidate can make it.
- **Flags doubt early.** A hesitant answer is noted in the summary so a recruiter can follow up.
- **Offers reschedules.** If the answer is no, the agent offers alternatives from Google Calendar and rebooks.
- **Gives practical details.** Address, parking, who to ask for, what to bring and how long it will take.
- **Catches changes of mind.** A candidate who has accepted elsewhere can say so, freeing the slot for another person.
- **Retries when unreached.** Up to 5 attempts in the retry hours you set, with not-reached analytics showing who never answered.
- **Separates risk levels.** Summaries let you sort confirmed, uncertain and unreachable candidates in minutes.

## Timing and content

| Moment | Call | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| After booking | Optional short confirmation | Catch wrong details early |
| Day before | Main reminder call | Confirm and offer reschedule |
| Morning of (start days) | Brief check | Catch last-minute problems |
| After a no-show | Recruiter, not agent | Understand why, decide what to do |

## A sample call

Illustrative.

**Agent:** Hello, this is an AI assistant from the recruitment team. I am calling to confirm your interview tomorrow at 10 am at the Leeds office. Are you still able to attend?
**Candidate:** Yes, I will be there.
**Agent:** Good. The office is on the second floor; ask for reception when you arrive. Do you need directions or parking information?
**Candidate:** Where do I park?
**Agent:** There is visitor parking behind the building, entrance on the side road. Anything else you would like to ask?
**Candidate:** Actually, I am not sure I can make ten. Could it be later?
**Agent:** I can offer eleven thirty tomorrow or ten on Thursday. Which would you prefer?
**Candidate:** Eleven thirty.
**Agent:** Rebooked for eleven thirty tomorrow. Thank you.

## What to set up first

1. **Pull the list of upcoming slots** daily or weekly, by interview and by start date.
2. **Write a short script.** Under a minute, one question, one offer. Long scripts lose people.
3. **Connect Google Calendar** so rebooking uses real availability. See [interview scheduling calls](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-interview-scheduling).
4. **Add logistics to the knowledge base:** address, parking, public transport, dress code and what to bring.
5. **Set calling hours and retries.** Late-evening calls hurt goodwill; choose a window and keep to it.
6. **Enable consent confirmation and AI disclosure.**
7. **Define what a recruiter reviews.** Anyone who is unsure, unreachable or cancelled goes onto a short follow-up list.

## Where it falls short

A reminder call reduces forgetfulness and logistical failures; it does not change a candidate's mind. A person who has quietly decided to take another job may still say yes and not appear. Calls are not a substitute for a good candidate experience earlier in the process, such as clear communication and a reasonable timeline. Over-calling can backfire, so keep to one or two touches. For first days, a human welcome and a clear contact name matter more than any call.
