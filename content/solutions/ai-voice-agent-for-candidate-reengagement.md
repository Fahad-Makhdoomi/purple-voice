Candidate re-engagement calls turn a dormant ATS into a source of fresh submissions. An AI voice agent phones past applicants and silver-medallist candidates, checks whether they are open to work now and records what they want, so recruiters call back only the ones who are live. It is aimed at recruiters sitting on thousands of records and under pressure to fill roles without paying again for sourcing.

## The database you already paid for

Every agency and in-house team has the same asset: thousands of candidates who applied, interviewed or were shortlisted and then went quiet. Many have since changed jobs, and some are now looking again. The records exist, but calling them is nobody's priority because each call has a low hit rate and takes recruiter time.

Voice suits this problem because the economics invert. An agent can make hundreds of low-yield calls without fatigue, and recruiters spend their effort only on the minority who say yes. It sits alongside [reactivating old leads with AI calls](/blog/reactivate-old-leads-with-ai-calls), applied to candidates rather than prospects.

## What an AI voice agent does for candidate re-engagement

- **Reintroduces your agency.** It reminds the candidate who you are and how they came to be in your database.
- **Asks whether they are open to work.** A simple yes, maybe or no, in their own words.
- **Updates the facts.** Current job title, preferred location, notice period, salary range and willingness to relocate are captured.
- **Handles questions about live roles.** From your knowledge base, it can describe vacancies that match.
- **Respects opt-outs.** A candidate who asks not to be called again is noted in the summary, so you can update the record.
- **Books recruiter time.** Interested candidates can choose a slot on Google Calendar.
- **Flags changed circumstances.** Retired, relocated or retrained candidates are easy to spot in the summaries.

## Segment, script, outcome

| Segment | Opening angle | Likely outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Silver medallists from a recent search | We liked your profile; a similar role is open | Highest interest, book straight in |
| Active applicants from the last year | Checking whether your situation has changed | Mixed; some roles filled, some open |
| Contractors whose assignment ended | Ask about next availability | Short, practical calls |
| Candidates older than two years | Ask for permission to keep their details | Many wrong numbers; use as a data clean |

## A sample call

Illustrative. Details are generic.

**Agent:** Hello, this is an AI assistant calling for the recruitment team. You spoke with us about a finance role last spring. Is now a good time for a minute?
**Candidate:** Oh, yes, I remember. I got another job at the time.
**Agent:** Thank you for telling me. Are you still there, or open to hearing about new roles?
**Candidate:** Still there, but I would listen. It has been a long year.
**Agent:** That is useful. Would you prefer to stay in the same area, or are you open to a different location?
**Candidate:** Same area, hybrid if possible.
**Agent:** I will record that. Would you like a recruiter to call you this week with relevant roles?
**Candidate:** Thursday afternoon would be good.
**Agent:** Booked for Thursday afternoon. Thank you.

## What to set up first

1. **Start with a recent, focused segment.** Silver medallists for one job family are the best first test.
2. **Check consent and source.** Confirm how the numbers were collected and what candidates agreed to. See [recruitment call compliance](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-recruitment-compliance).
3. **Write a short, honest opener.** Say who you are, say it is an AI, and give them an easy way to decline.
4. **Prepare the knowledge base** with current vacancies, so the agent does not invent roles.
5. **Set calling hours and retries,** up to 5. Do not call at unsociable times.
6. **Plan how opt-outs reach your ATS.** The summary lists them; someone needs to update the records.
7. **Test-call your own phone** with a reluctant and an enthusiastic persona.

## Where it falls short

Old data decays. Numbers change hands, and a share of calls will reach strangers or voicemails. An agent cannot fix a weak relationship: if candidates remember a poor experience with your process, the call will not repair it. It should not promise roles that do not exist, and it is not a replacement for a personal approach to senior candidates. Keep the volume proportionate; a candidate called repeatedly stops answering any of your numbers.
