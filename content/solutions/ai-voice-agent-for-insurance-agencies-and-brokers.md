An AI voice agent for insurance brokers takes the repeatable first calls of the day, such as enquiry callbacks, renewal reminders and out-of-hours messages, and gives your producers a summary and a booked slot instead of a missed call. It is a good fit for independent agencies with a handful of staff, where the person who sells is also the person who answers the phone.

The easiest way to see where it belongs is to follow a broker's day. This page does that, then covers setup. For the wider picture, start at the [insurance overview](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-insurance).

## The broker's day with an AI voice agent

| Time | What happens today | What the agent can take | What stays human |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before opening | Overnight web enquiries sit unread | Calls them as the office opens, confirms the request | Anything needing a quote built |
| Mid-morning | Producers are on calls, new leads wait | Answers inbound overflow, books callbacks | Existing clients with problems |
| Lunch | Phones go to voicemail | Takes messages with a reason and urgency | Urgent claims escalation |
| Afternoon | Renewal list gets worked in gaps | Calls renewal contacts, books review time | Price negotiations |
| Evening | Nobody answers | Inbound capture, see [after-hours calls](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-insurance-after-hours) | Emergencies |

## What an AI voice agent does for insurance brokers

- **Calls back new enquiries.** It reaches web-form and aggregator leads while they are still looking, which matters most for [auto insurance leads](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-auto-insurance-leads).
- **Routes by line of business.** Commercial, personal and benefits enquiries go to different producers' diaries.
- **Works the renewal list.** It chases the contacts nobody has had time to ring. The mechanics are on the [policy renewals page](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-policy-renewals).
- **Answers admin questions.** Opening hours, how to request a certificate, where to send documents, taken from the knowledge base.
- **Captures claim basics.** It collects the details and flags urgent ones, as described in [claims intake](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-claims-intake).
- **Keeps your CRM story straight.** Every call has a transcript and a summary, so the next person picking up the file knows what was said.
- **Never fills the diary blindly.** It books only slots you leave open on Google Calendar.

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

**Agent:** Hi, this is the automated assistant at a small independent insurance agency. You enquired about cover for your delivery van. Do you have two minutes?
**Caller:** Yes, go ahead.
**Agent:** How many vehicles are you looking to insure, and is it for your own business use?
**Caller:** Two vans, business use.
**Agent:** Thank you. Our commercial adviser handles fleet cover. I can book a call for tomorrow. Would the morning suit?
**Caller:** Can you just tell me the price?
**Agent:** I cannot quote prices myself. The adviser can once they have the full picture. Shall I book ten o'clock?
**Caller:** Fine.

## What to set up first

1. **Separate agents or sections for personal and commercial** if your producers specialise. One prompt per line of business keeps answers tight.
2. **Fill the knowledge base** with certificate procedures, document checklists and who handles what.
3. **Connect each producer's Google Calendar** and agree how many slots to leave open.
4. **Set campaign hours** to match your clients, with up to five retries for enquiry lists.
5. **Enable warm transfer** to the duty producer for ready-to-buy callers.
6. **Test with your own phone**, then ask a colleague to play a difficult client.
7. **Agree a review habit.** Ten minutes each morning on summaries is enough early on.

## Where it falls short

Brokers sell on trust, and trust comes from knowing the client's situation. The agent will not catch the nuance behind a vague answer, and it cannot place risk or negotiate terms. Long-standing clients may prefer their account manager's direct line, so keep that route open and do not point your best accounts at the agent first. If your enquiry volume is only a few a week, a simple callback routine may serve you as well.

Regulatory conduct rules for brokers differ by jurisdiction.
