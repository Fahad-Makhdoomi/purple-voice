AI policy renewal calls give every customer a personal reminder before their renewal date and surface the ones who are likely to leave. The agent asks whether anything has changed, books a review for those who want one and leaves your team with a short summary instead of a long list to ring. It suits personal-lines agencies and any team with a renewal book larger than the time to call it.

## Why renewals slip

Most agencies rely on a renewal notice, sent automatically, and the hope that customers act. The ones who need attention are the ones who do not say anything: the customer whose circumstances changed, the one comparing prices and the one who is annoyed about last year's claim. A call surfaces these. A notice does not.

The limit is time. A team might have a few hundred renewals a month and room to call a fraction of them. An agent can call all of them, and each takes minutes of your time only when something needs it.

## What an AI voice agent does on renewal calls

- **Reaches the whole list.** Every customer in the window gets a call, not only the large accounts.
- **Confirms the date.** A clear reminder of when the policy ends.
- **Asks the change question.** New address, new vehicle, new driver, a renovation, a business change.
- **Hears out concerns.** Price worries and service complaints are noted in the customer's words.
- **Books reviews.** Customers who want one are offered a slot on Google Calendar.
- **Routes simple needs.** Directions for paying, updating contact details or finding documents come from the knowledge base.
- **Flags retention risk.** The summary calls out those who said they would look elsewhere. See [lapsed policy win-back](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-lapsed-policy-winback) for what happens if they leave anyway.

## Before and after

| Stage | Notice only | With renewal calls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer awareness | A letter or automated note | A personal call a few weeks ahead |
| Changes in circumstances | Found when something goes wrong | Asked and noted before renewal |
| At-risk customers | Unknown until they lapse | Flagged with their stated reason |
| Staff time | Chasing at the last minute | Calling only flagged customers |
| Record of contact | Patchy | Transcript and summary per call |

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

**Agent:** Hello, this is an automated assistant calling for your insurance agency. Your home policy renews on the twelfth of next month. I wanted to check that your details are still right. Is now a good moment?
**Caller:** Go on.
**Agent:** Has anything changed, such as work done on the house or someone moving in or out?
**Caller:** We did build an extension in the summer.
**Agent:** Thank you, that is worth passing on, because it can affect your cover. Would you like someone from the team to review it with you before renewal?
**Caller:** Yes, please.
**Agent:** I can book a call on Wednesday afternoon. Does that work?

## What to set up first

1. **Define the renewal window.** Decide how many weeks ahead you call, then build the list from your management system.
2. **Write the prompt** with the policy types, your AI disclosure and a firm line on price: the agent never quotes a renewal premium.
3. **Fill the knowledge base** with how to pay, how to contact the team and where documents are kept.
4. **Set hours and up to five retries**, with an evening retry for working households.
5. **Connect Google Calendar** for review slots, and mark them as reviews so the right person is booked.
6. **Remove customers** who have already renewed or asked not to be called.
7. **Test on your own phone** with a made-up complaint to see how the summary reads.

## Where it falls short

The agent cannot discuss premiums unless you build a rating link, and it should not negotiate. It may fail to hear a quiet frustration that an account manager would pick up. Customers who see the call as a sales push will say so, and you should make it easy to decline. Some renewal conversations, such as after a claim, need a person from the first minute. Do not put those customers on the list.

Renewal calls to existing customers can fall under different rules from prospect calls, and insurance conduct rules vary. This is general information, not legal advice; check current requirements. See [disclosing AI on calls](/blog/disclose-ai-on-calls).
