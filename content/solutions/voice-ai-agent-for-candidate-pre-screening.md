Candidate pre-screening AI automates the first phone screen: a short call to every applicant that confirms the basics and gives a recruiter something concrete to review. If your inbox fills faster than you can dial, a voice agent can make those calls the same day applications arrive. This page is for in-house talent teams and small agencies who run structured screens and want them done consistently.

## What a voice AI agent does for candidate pre-screening

- **Calls fast.** The first screen goes out while the applicant is still thinking about the job, which is when people actually pick up.
- **Confirms the facts.** Location, availability, notice period, salary range, work authorisation and the one or two skills the role cannot do without.
- **Explains the role.** Pulls pay band, hours, location and process from your knowledge base so candidates are not left guessing.
- **Handles candidates' own questions.** "Is it remote?" and "What are the hours?" get answered; anything not in the knowledge base is noted for a human reply.
- **Books interviews.** Candidates who clear your criteria get a slot on your Google Calendar, or a warm transfer to a recruiter if one is free.
- **Records everything.** Transcript, AI summary and recording per call, plus not-reached analytics so you know who needs another route.

## Call type, agent action, handoff

| Situation | What the agent does | Who takes over |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate meets all stated criteria | Offers an interview slot | Recruiter, at the booked time |
| Candidate misses a stated requirement | Closes politely, flags it in the summary | Recruiter reviews before any rejection |
| Candidate asks to speak to a person | Notes the request, offers a callback or transfer | Recruiter |
| Candidate is unsure or gives unclear answers | Repeats once, then records "unclear" | Recruiter listens to that part |
| No answer | Retries on your schedule | You, if still not reached |

## A sample call

*Illustrative only.*

**Agent:** Hi, this is an AI assistant calling for the hiring team about your application for the warehouse supervisor role. Do you have a few minutes?

**Caller:** Yes, go ahead.

**Agent:** Great. The role is on nights, Sunday to Thursday. Does that pattern work for you?

**Caller:** I've done nights before. Sundays are tricky, though.

**Agent:** Understood. Could you do Sundays if given notice, or is it a firm no?

**Caller:** Probably not every week.

**Agent:** Thanks, I'll note that. Have you led a team of five or more before?

**Caller:** Yes, eight people at my last site.

**Agent:** Thank you. I'll pass this to the team, and someone will confirm next steps.

Notice that the agent does not reject on the Sunday answer. It records it and lets a recruiter weigh it.

## What to set up first

1. **Define what "qualified" means in writing.** Three must-haves and three nice-to-haves. Anything vaguer produces vague summaries.
2. **Write the prompt around that list.** Tone, order, what counts as an answer, when to move on. Use the [prompt template](/resources/ai-voice-agent-prompt-template).
3. **Load the knowledge base.** Role description, pay, shifts, benefits and the hiring steps.
4. **Set campaign hours.** Early evening often reaches working candidates better than mid-morning; set retries up to five at spaced hours.
5. **Connect Google Calendar.** Block recruiter availability so booked slots are real.
6. **Add consent confirmation.** The agent states it is an AI and checks the candidate is happy to continue.
7. **Test on your own phone.** Play a nervous candidate, a rude one and one who rambles.

## Where it falls short

- It judges answers, not people. Enthusiasm, presence and judgement show up in a live conversation, not a screening script.
- Some candidates dislike talking to a machine. Offer a human route and do not hide the AI.
- Hiring decisions can carry legal obligations in many places. This is general information, not legal advice; review current rules for your region.
- Poorly written criteria become poorly filtered candidates. The agent follows your script, including its flaws.
