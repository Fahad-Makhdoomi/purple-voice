An AI voice agent for RPO firms runs candidate calls across several client programmes without mixing scripts, numbers or reporting. Each client gets its own agent with its own tone, role details and knowledge base, and the RPO team sees everything in one dashboard. It is for operations and delivery leads who run recruitment on behalf of clients and need consistency without hiring a larger call team.

## The multi-client challenge

RPO teams carry brand risk that an ordinary agency does not. A candidate expects the call to sound like the employer, not a generic recruiter. Each client has its own process, its own questions and its own tolerance for risk. When screening is done by one shared team, consistency depends on training and good habits, and it shifts with attrition.

A voice agent configured per client makes the process repeatable. The script lives in the prompt, the facts live in the knowledge base, and the behaviour does not change because someone was off sick.

## What an AI voice agent does for RPO firms

- **Runs a separate agent per client.** Prompt, voice, knowledge and tone are specific to that employer's programme.
- **Calls under the right number.** Each client can have its own phone number, so call-backs reach the correct programme.
- **Screens to client criteria.** Each agent asks only that client's questions, in that client's order.
- **Books to the client's interview calendars.** Slots come from Google Calendar for the relevant hiring managers.
- **Produces client-ready summaries.** Calls, AI summaries and transcripts are organised by campaign for reporting.
- **Feeds your own systems.** On Pro, webhooks and API keys let you pass call outcomes into your ATS or reporting layer.
- **Scales with requisitions.** New roles mean a new campaign, not a new team.

## What to set up first

1. **Agree the script with each client.** Get sign-off on the opening, the questions and the AI disclosure before a single call goes out.
2. **Create one agent per programme,** with a clear name so your team does not run the wrong campaign.
3. **Fill each knowledge base** with that client's role facts, benefits, location and process, kept separate.
4. **Assign numbers.** Use a number per client where you can; the number rental is billed by your phone provider, not Purple Voice.
5. **Plan reporting.** Decide what each client receives and how often, and use webhooks on Pro for automation.
6. **Set campaign hours and retries,** up to 5, per client's requirements.
7. **Test-call your own phone** for every agent, and have a client contact do the same.

## Where it falls short

Running many clients is an operational discipline, not just a tool. A mis-pointed list or a stale knowledge base creates errors that are visible to each client's candidates. The agent also cannot resolve conflicts between client processes: if two clients want contradictory screening, they need two agents and clear boundaries. Clients who want a human voice throughout will not accept an agent for all stages, so be clear about where it appears. Keep humans accountable for shortlist decisions and for how candidate data is stored.
