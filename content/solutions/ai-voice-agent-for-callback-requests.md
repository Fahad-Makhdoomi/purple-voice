AI callback calls are outbound calls in which an agent rings back people who asked to be called, resolves simple questions on the spot, and books or transfers the rest to a person. They suit service desks, clinics, trades businesses and sales teams who collect callback requests faster than they can return them.

A callback request is a small promise. Kept within the hour, it works. Left until tomorrow, the person has often rung a competitor.

## What AI callback calls do

- **Ring promptly.** The agent calls within the hours you set, not when someone finds a gap in their day.
- **Open with context.** It states why it is calling and that it is an AI assistant.
- **Resolve simple requests.** Opening hours, a policy, a price band or a booking.
- **Offer a human slot.** If a person is needed, it books a time on Google Calendar.
- **Retry politely.** Up to five attempts with retry hours that you choose.
- **Hand over the context.** An AI summary and transcript save the person from starting cold.

## Manual callbacks versus AI callbacks

| Factor | Manual callbacks | AI callback calls |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | When someone is free | Within minutes, inside calling hours |
| Retries | Often forgotten | Up to five, tracked |
| Notes | Written if there is time | Summary on every call |
| Evenings | Rarely covered | Within your calling hours |
| Complex issues | Person handles | Booked or transferred to a person |

## A sample call

An illustrative script with no real business.

Agent: Hello, is that Dan? This is an AI assistant calling from the service desk because you asked for a callback about your boiler service. Is now a good moment?

Caller: Yes, go ahead.

Agent: Thanks. You asked about booking an annual service. Our engineers have an opening on Wednesday morning or Thursday afternoon. Which would you like?

Caller: Wednesday is fine.

Agent: I have booked Wednesday morning and noted your address. You also mentioned a noise from the boiler. I cannot diagnose that, so I will pass it to the engineer on the job. Is the noise happening every day?

Caller: Mostly in the evening.

Agent: Noted. Thank you, Dan.

## What to set up first

1. **Decide the response window.** If your promise is the same day, set the calling hours to match.
2. **Gather the data.** Name, number, reason and when the request was made.
3. **Write the callback opening.** Say it is an AI, state why you are calling and ask if it is a good moment.
4. **Define what it may resolve.** And what must go to a person. See [AI-to-human handoff](/solutions/ai-voice-agent-for-customer-care-handoff).
5. **Connect Google Calendar.** For human callback slots or service bookings.
6. **Confirm consent.** The campaign asks you to confirm it. [TCPA and AI voice calls](/blog/tcpa-and-ai-voice-calls) has general notes.
7. **Test on your own phone.** Include a person who says it is a bad time and one who asks for a human.

## Where it falls short

Callbacks fail when the number is wrong, the person is no longer interested or the request was vague. The agent cannot fix that, and some people will hang up on an unexpected call.

It also cannot resolve complex issues. A good callback agent knows when to stop, book a human and summarise accurately. If your team does not act on the booked slots, the process gains speed but not outcomes.
