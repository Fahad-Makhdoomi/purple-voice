An ai voice agent for solar sales calls homeowners who have enquired, checks whether they own the property and whether solar is likely to suit it, and books a survey. The sales team then drives to qualified homes instead of speculative ones. It is aimed at installers and solar lead teams where a wasted visit costs a half day.

## What an AI voice agent does for solar sales

- **Calls new enquiries quickly,** often outside office hours when homeowners are free to talk.
- **Confirms ownership.** Renters and landlords have different permissions, so this question comes early.
- **Checks the roof basics.** Roof type, rough age and whether it is shaded, using the wording your surveyors use.
- **Asks about energy spend,** such as a rough monthly bill, to judge fit.
- **Explains the next step.** What a survey involves, how long it takes and what the homeowner should have ready.
- **Books the survey** on Google Calendar with address and contact details.
- **Sends the surveyor a summary** and a transcript so nothing is asked twice.

## Qualify, disqualify, escalate

| Homeowner says | Agent response | Result |
|---|---|---|
| "I own it, south-facing roof" | Continues, asks about bill | Likely qualified |
| "I rent" | Thanks them, explains it needs the owner | Closed politely |
| "Not sure about the roof" | Notes it, books a survey anyway | Surveyor checks |
| "How much will it cost?" | Explains a survey comes first | Booking offered |
| "I already got quotes" | Asks what they want clarified | Rep review |

## A sample call

Illustrative only.

> **Agent:** Hello, this is the assistant from a solar installation company. You asked for information on rooftop panels.
> **Caller:** Yes, a few days ago.
> **Agent:** Thanks. Do you own the property?
> **Caller:** Yes, we have lived here ten years.
> **Agent:** Is the roof mainly tiles, or something else?
> **Caller:** Tiles, facing south-west, I think.
> **Agent:** And roughly what do you spend on electricity each month?
> **Caller:** Around 140.
> **Agent:** That is helpful. A surveyor can visit and check the roof properly. Would Wednesday morning or Friday afternoon suit you?
> **Caller:** Friday afternoon.
> **Agent:** Booked. I will call the day before to confirm.

## What to set up first

1. **List the disqualifiers** such as renters, shaded roofs or unsupported roof types, and decide which end the call and which still get a visit.
2. **Write the qualifying script** in plain language, no jargon about inverters or kilowatt peaks unless the caller uses it.
3. **Add a knowledge base** with what you install, areas you cover, how surveys work and approved statements about incentives that apply today.
4. **Connect Google Calendar** for surveyors with travel buffers.
5. **Set calling hours** that suit households, with up to 5 retries.
6. **Create a reminder campaign** for the day before each survey.
7. **Test on your own phone,** with a renter, a sceptic and someone who asks for a price.

## Keeping ai voice agent for solar sales honest

Solar attracts exaggerated claims. Give the agent only statements your company stands behind, avoid guaranteed savings and tell the caller that a survey decides what is possible. Funding schemes, tariffs and grants change, so keep that knowledge base entry current or leave it out.

## Where it falls short

- **Pricing.** The agent should not quote unless you provide approved ranges.
- **Technical judgement.** Roof suitability, shading and structural questions belong to a surveyor.
- **Incentives and finance.** These vary by place and change often; refer to a human adviser.
- **Pushback.** The agent should accept a no and not pressure homeowners.
- **Door-to-door style lists.** Cold calling households is restricted in many regions. Check current rules; this is general information, not legal advice.
